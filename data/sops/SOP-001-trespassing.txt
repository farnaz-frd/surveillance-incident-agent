SOP ID: SOP-001
Title: Trespassing Response and Perimeter Breach Protocol
Category: trespassing
Last Reviewed: 2026-01-15

1. SCOPE
This procedure applies to any detected presence of unauthorized individuals within
restricted perimeter zones, including fence lines, loading docks after operating
hours, and rooftop access points.

2. IMMEDIATE ACTIONS
2.1 Upon detection with confidence >= 0.6, the on-duty security operator must be
    alerted within 60 seconds via the incident dashboard.
2.2 Cross-reference the individual's presence against the badge access log for the
    zone and time window. If no valid badge scan exists within a 10-minute window
    of detection, escalate to Level 2.
2.3 If the individual is near a marked hazard area (chemical storage, electrical
    substation, construction zone), escalate immediately to Level 3 regardless of
    badge status.

3. ESCALATION LEVELS
Level 1 (Confidence 0.6-0.75, no hazard proximity): Log incident, notify shift
  supervisor, continue monitoring. No dispatch required unless individual remains
  after 5 minutes.
Level 2 (No badge match found): Dispatch on-site security personnel to the zone.
  Notify facility manager. Begin continuous recording retention for the incident.
Level 3 (Hazard proximity OR after-hours breach at critical infrastructure):
  Immediate dispatch, notify facility manager AND regional security director,
  initiate lockdown protocol for adjacent zones if applicable.

4. DOCUMENTATION REQUIREMENTS
Every trespassing incident, regardless of level, requires: timestamp, camera ID,
badge log cross-reference result, entities detected, and final disposition
(cleared / escalated / law enforcement contacted).

5. FALSE POSITIVE HANDLING
Wildlife, maintenance staff without updated badge records, and delivery personnel
are common false positive sources. If badge log shows a valid maintenance work
order for the zone/time, downgrade to informational log only.
