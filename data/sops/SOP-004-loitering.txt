SOP ID: SOP-004
Title: Loitering Assessment Procedure
Category: loitering
Last Reviewed: 2025-12-10

1. SCOPE
Applies to individuals remaining in a monitored zone beyond expected dwell time
without a clear operational purpose — entrances, parking structures, loading
areas, and perimeter zones are the most common contexts.

2. IMMEDIATE ACTIONS
2.1 Loitering is inherently a lower-confidence, higher-ambiguity category than
    trespassing. Do not escalate on detection alone; require a minimum
    persistence window of 3 minutes across multiple sampled frames before
    treating as actionable.
2.2 Cross-reference badge scan logs — an individual loitering near an entrance
    they are badged for (e.g. waiting for a colleague) is a low-priority case.
2.3 Loitering in a parking structure or perimeter zone after standard business
    hours warrants closer review than the same behavior during business hours.

3. ESCALATION LEVELS
Level 1 (Business hours, near a zone the individual has valid badge access to):
  Log only, no action.
Level 2 (After hours OR no badge correlation, persistence > 5 minutes):
  Notify shift supervisor, flag for operator visual review via live feed if
  available.
Level 3 (After hours, no badge correlation, near a Tier-1 critical zone as
  defined in SOP-002, persistence > 10 minutes): Dispatch security to assess.

4. DOCUMENTATION REQUIREMENTS
Persistence duration, badge correlation result, time-of-day context, and
zone classification.
