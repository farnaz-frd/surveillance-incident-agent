SOP ID: SOP-003
Title: Physical Hazard Detection and Reporting Protocol
Category: hazard
Last Reviewed: 2025-11-20

1. SCOPE
Applies to detected physical hazards including spills, fire/smoke indicators,
structural damage, blocked emergency exits, and equipment left in unsafe states
(e.g. forklift left running, chemical containers left open).

2. IMMEDIATE ACTIONS
2.1 Any hazard detection with confidence >= 0.5 triggers an automatic check
    against the maintenance schedule tool for the affected zone — an open
    maintenance work order may explain the condition (e.g. active cleaning,
    known repair in progress).
2.2 If no matching maintenance work order exists, classify as an unplanned
    hazard and proceed to severity assessment.
2.3 Fire, smoke, or structural collapse indicators bypass all other steps and
    trigger Level 3 escalation immediately, regardless of maintenance schedule.

3. ESCALATION LEVELS
Level 1 (Matches an open maintenance work order): Log as informational,
  no further action required.
Level 2 (Unplanned hazard, non-life-safety — e.g. spill, blocked non-emergency
  walkway): Notify facilities team, log for same-shift resolution, re-check
  in 30 minutes if unresolved.
Level 3 (Fire/smoke, structural damage, blocked emergency exit, or hazard in
  a high-traffic zone): Immediate notification to facility manager and safety
  officer. Blocked emergency exits require notification within 2 minutes
  regardless of time of day.

4. DOCUMENTATION REQUIREMENTS
Maintenance schedule cross-reference result, hazard type, zone traffic level
at time of detection, and time-to-resolution once flagged.
