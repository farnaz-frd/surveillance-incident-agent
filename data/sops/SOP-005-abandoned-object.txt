SOP ID: SOP-005
Title: Abandoned Object Response Procedure
Category: abandoned_object
Last Reviewed: 2026-01-05

1. SCOPE
Applies to any object (bag, package, container) detected as stationary and
separated from any individual for longer than the persistence threshold, in
any monitored zone.

2. IMMEDIATE ACTIONS
2.1 Confirm persistence: the object must remain in place, unattended, across
    at least 3 consecutive sampled frames (approx. 6 minutes at default
    sampling rate) before being treated as actionable. Single-frame detections
    are very frequently false positives (e.g. a person briefly out of frame).
2.2 Cross-reference maintenance schedule tool — some zones have legitimately
    placed equipment (cleaning carts, delivery pallets) that may be
    misclassified.
2.3 Objects in high-traffic public-facing areas (lobbies, transit points) are
    treated with higher urgency than objects in low-traffic back-of-house areas.

3. ESCALATION LEVELS
Level 1 (Matches known maintenance/delivery equipment placement): Log only.
Level 2 (Unexplained, low-traffic zone, persistence 6-15 minutes): Notify
  facilities/security for physical inspection, no evacuation.
Level 3 (Unexplained, high-traffic or public-facing zone, persistence > 15
  minutes, OR any unexplained object near critical infrastructure): Notify
  facility manager and safety officer immediately. Consider area clearance
  pending physical inspection, per site's standard bomb-threat-adjacent
  caution procedures.

4. DOCUMENTATION REQUIREMENTS
Persistence duration, zone traffic classification, maintenance schedule
cross-reference result, and inspection outcome once resolved.
